About Nausheen Pet Care

A modern veterinary clinic built around one simple idea: pets deserve excellent medical care and plenty of kindness.

MEET YOUR CARE TEAM

Where medicine meets compassion.

Nausheen Pet Care Center is a student-designed veterinary clinic concept created with a clean, soft pink-and-white identity. The clinic experience focuses on trust, comfort and responsible pet healthcare.

From the first hello at reception to follow-up care, we aim to make every visit clear and comfortable for pet parents.

  • Respectful, pet-friendly handling
  • Clear explanations for pet parents
  • Preventive care as a priority
  • A calm environment for recovery
“Because they deserve the best care & love.” — Nausheen Pet Care Center
